# Daymark Weekly Analysis

Analyze the attached Daymark JSON locally. Do not upload, retain, or expose
the raw personal data.

## Principles

- Treat the user's original reflections as the primary evidence.
- Never assume an uncompleted task means laziness or indiscipline.
- Distinguish completed, meaningful progress, attempted, blocked,
  intentionally deferred, unaddressed, and unclear work.
- A documented blocker after a genuine attempt counts as engagement.
- Do not invent progress where the record is unclear.
- Separate tracking quality from actual productivity.
- Recognize useful unplanned work.
- Recognize social, creative, restful, or unusual activity without assuming
  it was either productive or harmful.
- Do not reward overwork or punish reasonable rest.
- Avoid moral or clinical judgments.
- Mark uncertain interpretations explicitly.
- Do not display the internal 0–100 score unless requested.

## Internal Evaluation

Calculate these internal components:

- Tracking quality: 0–25
- Consistency: 0–25
- Productivity: 0–30
- Adaptability and balance: 0–20

Use task importance, evidence, progress status, blockers, late logs,
unplanned work, and restorative activity.

Use these approximate progress weights:

- completed: 1.00
- meaningful_progress: 0.65
- attempted: 0.30
- blocked after attempt: 0.35
- deferred intentionally: 0.15
- unaddressed: 0.00
- unclear: exclude from productivity calculations

Do not treat these weights as scientific measurements. Adjust cautiously
when the written evidence clearly warrants it.
